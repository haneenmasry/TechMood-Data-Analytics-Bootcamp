# Lesson 01: Data Analytics Mindset & Workflow

## 📊 Overview
This repository contains the deliverables for Lesson 01 of the TechMood Data Analytics Bootcamp. The focus of this lesson is to practice analytical thinking, understand the data lifecycle, and transform real-world business challenges into data-driven solutions.

---

## 🏥 Project 1: Hospital Patient Flow Bottleneck Analysis
* **Business Problem:** A 35% increase in average patient waiting times over the last 4 months, causing emergency room overcrowding and a 14% rate of patients leaving without being seen (LWBS).
* **Objective:** Identify throughput bottlenecks using operational timestamps and optimize patient flow.
* **Key Insights:** Waiting times are highly impacted by data-driven operational scheduling and patient psychology. Keeping patients informed reduces perceived wait times and anxiety.
* **Proposed Decisions:** Implemented a Fast-Track system for low-acuity cases and demand-based staffing shifts.

---

## 🧠 Project 2: Careem Case Study
* **Objective:** Analyze how Careem leverages the data lifecycle to optimize its platform mechanics.
* **Actionable Solution:** Developed the **Predictive Pre-Matching** strategy, which cross-references routine user behaviors with live weather and event data to match drivers with passengers *before* peak demand spikes.

---

## 🛒 Challenge: E-commerce Abandoned Carts Analysis
- **Problem:** 74% cart abandonment rate at the checkout stage.
- **Analysis & Decision:** Addressed "Transaction Shock" by embedding shipping costs into product pricing (Free Shipping psychology) and integrating 1-click payments (Apple Pay) to reduce checkout friction.
